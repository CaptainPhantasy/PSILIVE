## Confirmed lateral-lining requirements

The process covers rehabilitation of existing sanitary laterals. Pre-repair video must show all reused sections and problems clearly, include footage measurements, and record toward and away from the sanitary sewer. Poor recordings must be redone. Filename: address plus a before indication; no fixed separator/template is prescribed. The contractor uploads to its designated Box.com folder, then emails **REACH@CitizensEnergyGroup.com**.

After installation, the contractor uploads post-repair video and requests Citizens’ inspection online. The sheet does not separately prescribe post-video naming, recording directions, measurement origin, or file format; confirm those with the reviewer. Leave excavated trenches open for inspection. Owner/representative handles application and installation costs; connected-facility work requires Indianapolis/Marion County licensing and compliance with Citizens’ standards. PSI camera work does not replace these duties.

## 2026 standards manual — additional verified guidance

The earlier search extraction error was caused by the manual's 15.8 MB size. A direct download succeeded on October 10. Section **404.06, Pipe Lining on Lateral Repairs and Private Sewers**, on PDF page **105**, says lining is considered case by case and requires a utility-approved variance. The variance application identifies lining material, curing method, and the installation contractor with contact information. It prohibits styrene liners (the PDF spells this “Stryrene”). After variance approval, pre-construction inspection documents existing conditions and post-construction CCTV checks the work. Failed work requires repairs and a new inspection; videos must be available to the utility on request. These are installation/project obligations, not a claim of PSI approval or certification.

Source: https://info.citizensenergygroup.com/hubfs/site/permits%20and%20forms%20documents/2026%20Sanitary%20Standards%20Manual.pdf
