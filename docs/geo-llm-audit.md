Checked October 10, 2026. Baseline: published editing copy v12, source 94d63dc65194c2840ac18d8066d90b3b868fd217.

These are practical readiness scores from page content, generated HTML, and source review. They are not search rankings, measured traffic, citation counts, or guarantees of discovery. GEO means how ready a page is to support useful answers in generative search. Language-model visibility means how easily a system can find, understand, and accurately describe PSI.

The editing copy deliberately remains noindex and preserves the business site's canonical URLs. The improvements prepare the source for the authorized editing site. They do not change the production business domain or prove that search systems indexed it.

- Added direct answers covering required pre/post video, pre-video filenames, Box upload/email, post-video inspection requests, excavation, and unspecified recording details.
- Checked Citizens' current official index and lateral-lining sheet. Revision remains July 14, 2026.
- Downloaded the currently linked 320-page sanitary standards manual after web extraction rejected its size. Read the cover (January 2026 revision) and page 105, section 404.06. Added the approved-variance requirement and material/curing/installer information to the page.
- Added a separate official project resources area with clickable Citizens links and the October 10 check date. Kept source notes and publication checks separate from the service copy.
- Linked the quick guide to the preparation checklist, contractor answers, and PSI/Indiana Drain Company's relationship page.
- Preserved all supplied artwork and job-scope cautions. No fabricated employee, job, testimonial, accreditation, utility approval, turnaround, price, or service-area promise was added.
- Aligned the existing llms.txt summary with the public service pages: removed the unqualified fixed delivery and starting-price claims, used shared company contact facts, added the contractor page and verified Citizens resources, and clearly assigned project responsibilities. Kept price and documentation delivery subject to confirmed scope. Corrected its comment so it does not promise special AI discovery. This updates an existing file; it does not introduce a special discovery mechanism.
- Checked the completed shared business schema: one business identity, Nashville locality from existing company facts, stable phone/email, and cities represented as service coverage rather than invented branches. Verified crawler notes now distinguish search access from training. The companion SEO work also aligns metadata and removes unsupported schema claims.

## Evidence-based measurement after publication

Use the business domain's verified search tools to measure indexed pages, impressions, clicks, actual enquiries, and citation activity. Separate source improvements from evidence that platforms have crawled and used them. Bing's AI Performance report measures citations and cited pages; it does not report ranking or placement within an answer. Google provides its own search performance reporting. No account metrics were accessed or invented during this audit.

The highest remaining value is original evidence PSI can supply: approved real field photos, consented examples of redacted findings, clearly attributed expert review, and confirmed job-specific deliverables. Keep the facts-to-confirm list in the contractor research document; do not replace unknowns with marketing claims.

No special AI text file, hidden keyword block, fake review schema, or artificial mention campaign was added. Google says its generative search features rely on established search practices and helpful original content; special llms.txt files, special schema, and writing only for AI are unnecessary. OpenAI distinguishes OAI-SearchBot for search from GPTBot for possible model training; allowing training is not a search-placement guarantee.
