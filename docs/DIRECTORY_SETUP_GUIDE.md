# PSI Directory Listings Setup Guide

## Priority 1: Google Business Profile (Most Important)
**Impact:** 40% of local search visibility
**Time:** 30 minutes to set up, 1-2 weeks for verification

### Step 1: Go to Google Business Profile Manager
1. Visit: https://business.google.com
2. Sign in with your Google account (use booking@ or your main Google account)
3. Click "Manage Locations" or "Add Location"

### Step 2: Enter Your Business Information

**Business Name:** Precision Sewer Inspections
*(Use exactly this - matches your domain)*

**Category:** 
- Primary: "Sewer Cleaning Service"
- Secondary: "Plumber", "Drain Cleaning Service"

**Address:** Enter your physical address OR service area
*(For service businesses without a storefront, you can use "Service Area" mode)*

**Service Areas:** Add all the areas you serve:
- Indianapolis, IN
- Carmel, IN
- Fishers, IN
- Noblesville, IN
- Greenwood, IN
- Avon, IN
- Plainfield, IN
- Brownsburg, IN
- Zionsville, IN

### Step 3: Contact Information
- **Phone:** (317) XXX-XXXX *(Your actual number)*
- **Website:** https://precisionsewerinspections.com

### Step 4: Business Hours
```
Monday:    8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Tuesday:   8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Wednesday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Thursday:  8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Friday:    8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Saturday:  9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Sunday:    Closed
```

### Step 5: Add Photos (Very Important!)
Upload at minimum:
- [ ] Logo (square, 250x250 minimum)
- [ ] Cover/Header photo (1080x608 or larger)
- [ ] 3-5 photos of your truck with company branding
- [ ] 2-3 photos of equipment
- [ ] 2-3 photos of team members
- [ ] 1-2 photos of completed work (before/after if possible)

### Step 6: Complete Business Description
```
Precision Sewer Inspections provides professional sewer and drain camera 
inspection services for homeowners, realtors, and property managers 
throughout the Indianapolis metro area. Our InterNACHI-member inspector uses 
state-of-the-art camera technology to identify problems before they 
become emergencies.

Services include standard sewer inspections, pre-listing inspections for 
home sellers, and inspection-window scopes for real estate transactions.

Fully insured. Same-day service available.
```

### Step 7: Verify Ownership
Google will send a postcard to your address OR call/email to verify. This takes 1-2 weeks.

### Optimization Tips:
- Add your services in the "Services" section
- Set up messaging to receive text inquiries
- Enable Google Reviews auto-response
- Link to your Google Ads account if using

## Priority 2: Yelp for Business
**Impact:** 15% of local search visibility
**Time:** 20 minutes

### Step 1: Claim Your Business
1. Visit: https://biz.yelp.com
2. Search for your business name
3. If it exists, click "Claim This Business"
4. If not, click "Add Your Business to Yelp"

### Step 2: Complete Your Profile
- **Business Name:** Precision Sewer Inspections
- **Category:** Sewer Services
- **Contact:** Your phone and website
- **Hours:** Same as Google
- **Photos:** Upload same set as Google

### Step 3: Add Services
List all your service offerings with descriptions and pricing.

### Step 4: Yelp Ads (Optional but Recommended)
Yelp advertising can boost visibility. Start with $50-100/month to test.

---

## Priority 3: HomeAdvisor / Angi
**Impact:** Strong for home services referrals
**Time:** 30 minutes

### Step 1: Create Pro Account
1. Visit: https://www.homeadvisor.com/pro/
2. Or Angi: https://www.angi.com/business/

### Step 2: Complete Profile
- License information
- Insurance certificates
- Service areas
- Photos and descriptions

### Step 3: Leads Program
HomeAdvisor/Angi sells leads to homeowners. Cost varies by market. Worth testing for lead quality.

## Priority 4: Houzz Pro
**Impact:** Strong for homeowners and renovation professionals
**Time:** 20 minutes

### Step 1: Create Houzz Pro Account
1. Visit: https://www.houzz.com/pro/
2. Sign up for Houzz Pro

### Step 2: Set Up Profile
- Upload portfolio photos
- Complete service descriptions
- Set service areas
- Enable lead notifications

---

## Priority 5: BBB (Better Business Bureau)
**Impact:** Trust signals, appears in search results
**Time:** 30 minutes + $80-200/year

### Step 1: Apply for Accreditation
1. Visit: https://www.bbb.org/accreditation
2. Apply for your business

### Step 2: Complete Profile
- Business description
- License information
- Years in business
- Contact information

### Note: BBB accreditation has annual fees but provides trust badge for your website.
